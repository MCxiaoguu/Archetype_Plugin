Security & Legal

Your monitoring data describes how your infrastructure behaves — we treat it accordingly.

Security at Relay

Encryption

All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. SDK tokens are stored hashed; we can never display a token after creation.

Access control

Production access is limited to a small on-call group, gated by hardware-key MFA, and every access is logged. Customer data is never used for anything other than serving your workspace.

Compliance

Relay is SOC 2 Type II certified (report available under NDA) and GDPR compliant. EU customers can pin data residency to our Frankfurt region.

Reporting a vulnerability

Found something? Email security@relaymonitor.io. We respond within one business day and pay bounties for qualifying reports.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

We collect the minimum needed to operate the Service:

  • Account data — your name, email, and billing details, used for authentication and invoicing.
  • Monitoring data — the events, metrics, and check results your services send us, used solely to render your dashboards and alerts.
  • Usage data — aggregate, anonymized product analytics that help us improve Relay.

We never sell your data, and we never share it with third parties except the payment processor and cloud infrastructure providers necessary to run the Service. You can request export or deletion of your data at any time by emailing privacy@relaymonitor.io.

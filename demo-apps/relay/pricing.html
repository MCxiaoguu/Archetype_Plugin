Frequently asked questions

What counts as an event?

One tracked request, one custom Relay.track() call, or one synthetic check result. Alerts, timeline entries, and dashboard views are always free.

What happens if I go over my event quota?

We never drop data mid-month. On Free, ingestion pauses until the next cycle; on Pro, overage is billed at $1 per additional 10k events.

Can I change plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing; downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

Do you offer discounts for startups or open source?

Open-source projects get Pro free — email us a link to your repo. Early-stage startups (under $2M raised) get 50% off Pro for the first year.
